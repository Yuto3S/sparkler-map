Before you go

Stock changes during the day. Open the product page, choose your branch and check it shows "Verfügbar". "Wenige verfügbar" means only a few left.

On the balcony

Light them outdoors, hold them at arm's length, and keep a bucket of water for the hot wires. They stay hot for a while after the sparks stop.
